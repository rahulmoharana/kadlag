Our Philosophy & Genesis

Built on the conviction that clean spaces reflect humane, dignified partnerships.

Kadlag Facility Management Pvt. Ltd. was established to correct an industry disconnect: the commoditization of cleaning workers and reliance on harsh, volatile chemicals that compromised both ambient indoor air quality and human wellbeing.

We built a service model centered on three non-negotiables: Clean. Care. Comfort. By treating facility staff as master technicians—equipping them with rigorous multi-stage training, dignified compensation, and plant-based, enzyme-driven detergents—we deliver immaculate precision for high-density tech parks, healthcare centers, luxury residences, and retail flagships throughout India.
